Menstrual health, made simple.

Reliable, accessible information can help people make informed decisions and reduce stigma.

01

What is menstruation?

Menstruation is a normal biological process. Learning about it helps create healthier and more supportive communities.

02

Choosing menstrual products

Different people may prefer different products. Availability, comfort, affordability and personal needs all matter.

03

Hygiene & disposal

Good menstrual hygiene includes access to clean facilities, appropriate products and safe disposal options.

04

Breaking the stigma

Menstruation should be discussed respectfully and without shame. Everyone can contribute to more supportive communities.

05

Supporting someone

A small act of support, such as helping someone find a product, can make access easier when they need it.

06

When to seek help

If someone has menstrual symptoms that concern them or interfere significantly with daily life, they can consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.

About Pad Koi

Finding a menstrual product should not be unnecessarily difficult.

Pad Koi is a digital menstrual health accessibility initiative by ScriptySphere. The platform focuses on one practical problem: helping people discover where menstrual products may be available around them.

SDG 3 · Good Health SDG 5 · Gender Equality SDG 6 · Clean Water SDG 4 · Education

Our approach

Pad Koi combines location intelligence, community participation and menstrual health information into one accessible digital experience.


The platform does not replace healthcare services or solve every menstrual health barrier. Instead, it targets a specific gap: discoverability and navigation to potential sources of menstrual products.
